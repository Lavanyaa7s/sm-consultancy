Q1 Who can use SM Consultancy's services?

We work with sole proprietors, partnerships, Sendirian Berhad (Sdn. Bhd.) companies, fast-growing SMEs, startups, and individuals who require professional personal tax filing assistance across Melaka and nationwide.

Q2 What is the difference between Form BE and Form B?

Form BE is for individuals earning employment income only (due by 30 April). Form B is for individuals earning business, freelance, partnership, or trade income (due by 30 June). Our tax team will verify your exact position and calculate all allowable tax reliefs to minimize your final tax payable.

Q3 Do I need to register for Sales & Service Tax (SST)?

SST registration is required if your annual taxable turnover exceeds the statutory threshold (generally RM500,000 for most prescribed taxable services, or RM1,500,000 for food and beverage operators). We conduct an evaluation of your business invoices and manage your SST-01 registration and bi-monthly returns.

Q4 How can you help my company prepare for LHDN e-Invoice?

We provide full end-to-end e-Invoice support: reviewing your current invoice issuance workflow, setting up and authenticating your company on the LHDN MyInvois portal, integrating your accounting software or ERP/POS, and training your finance personnel to ensure 100% compliant e-Invoice generation.

Q5 Do you handle monthly payroll and statutory contributions?

Yes. Our payroll department processes gross-to-net salary computations, distributes itemized electronic payslips, manages monthly submissions for EPF, SOCSO, EIS, and prepares Monthly Tax Deductions (PCB), as well as annual EA forms for all staff.

Q6 How do I get started with SM Consultancy?

Getting started is simple. Simply send an inquiry via our contact form or message us directly on WhatsApp. We will arrange a free introductory discovery session, assess your requirements, and provide a clear, transparent scope and fixed quote with no hidden fees.
